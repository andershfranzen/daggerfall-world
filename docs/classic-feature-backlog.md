# What remains to make classic Daggerfall playable

**25 November 2026.** The largest remaining playthrough is the route from a new character through the royal courts, the Emperor’s letter and the seven endings. Later campaign checkpoints exercise individual branches. Ordinary quests also need branch-by-branch checks: 301 scripts pass static engine analysis, and the census names the smaller set exercised by a player or an installed-data fixture. For combat, all 61 supported enemy types have appeared, with 19 walked encounters recorded. [Campaign verification](#campaign-verification) · [Quest census](quest-census.md) · [Encounter roster](classic-combat-coverage.md)

Across all 15,251 locations in the installed classic map, **18,469 tavern buildings contain 121,270 fixed person placements**. Every tavern has at least one, and 84 have just one. These are building instances, including inns inside cities, so counting only map locations of type “tavern” misses most of them. In three settlements I checked separately, the indoor reader resolved all 169 people and all 26 innkeepers. In Blackidge Hamlet, a local directed a player to The Devil’s Dagger. The player walked to the door and the keeper. A second player walked to the same inn independently, and both saw all six people. After reconnect, the same six people and the inn’s 20 lamps were present. A separate Blackidge inn had eight occupants. [Reproducible census](../tools/classic-feature-gaps.mjs) · [Indoor people reader](../server/rpg/npcs/world.js)

Of 444,274 classic building records across the map, 54,282 contain no fixed person. Two house types (21 and 22) and generic building type 23 account for 53,738 of those. Every general store, bank, guild hall, temple and tavern has at least one fixed person in its source data. Opening hours and quest appearances determine who is actually there at a given time. [Building types and census](../tools/classic-feature-gaps.mjs)

The shared name directory resolves 38,937 building names from the installed classic map. That includes all 18,469 taverns, 7,303 general stores, 2,970 temples, 1,478 guild halls and 767 banks. The three royal palaces have individual names in TEXT.RSC. The other 319 use the classic “Palace” label. These are record counts; the Play checks below cover individual visits. [Building directory](../client/world/interiors/buildingDirectory.js) · [Reproducible census](../tools/classic-feature-gaps.mjs)

The baseline is the original PC game with the supplied ARENA2 files. The Daggerfall Unity sources help identify rules, record formats and player actions. Its optional enhancements, World of Daggerfall scenery and the multiplayer website have separate scope. “Missing” below means a classic player action still needs a gameplay route. “Partial” means part of that route works. “Verify” calls for a playthrough before scheduling new implementation.
