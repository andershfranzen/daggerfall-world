# Classic spells and the Spell Maker

The server reads the operator’s `SPELLS.STD` and uses the source-backed effect table. Package 9 keeps beneficial casts on players and hostile player casts on monsters. Enemy and dungeon-trap casts can affect players. Combat, inventory, guilds, conditions, collision and doors own their respective state. ## Installed records

The installed file yields 89 spell records, 12 hidden names and 91 classic effect templates. Both record 58 entries survive as `std:58` (Holy Word) and `std:58.1` (Holy Touch). The catalogue applies the reference correction to the first effect of record 10, Free Action. The hidden records are 71–81 and 92. Record 92 remains reserved to the lycanthrope’s transformation route. Record 66 is the combat-linked Spider Touch. ## Reference details and multiplayer adaptations

- Create Item stores the player’s game minute plus the rolled duration on each item. An item disappears on the first minute after that deadline, including after travel, rest or a reconnect. Expiry clears worn gear from its equipment slot. The reference implements the intended expiry and documents a DOS flag bug that could leave gear permanent. Transfers are refused, including dropping, selling, trade and storage. Conjured arrows come in quantities of 1–20, remain separate from ordinary ammunition and are spent from the earliest expiry first. Player arrows are currently unrecoverable in the shared combat path. [Reference lifetime](https://github.com/Interkarma/daggerfall-unity/blob/3d85a283672a4955e32992097ea7719e896dbadb/Assets/Scripts/Game/Items/ItemCollection.cs) · [Reference inventory transfer](https://github.com/Interkarma/daggerfall-unity/blob/3d85a283672a4955e32992097ea7719e896dbadb/Assets/Scripts/Game/UserInterfaceWindows/DaggerfallInventoryWindow.cs)
- Dispel Magic spends magicka when the picker opens. The player can cancel, dispel their own eligible spell automatically, or roll the spell’s chance against a foreign bundle. A bundle is eligible if an active component shows a spell icon. Potion effects, pending door magic, condition-managed effects and passive enchantment modifiers stay outside the picker. A worn item’s live Cast When Held spell is offered, as in the reference. Once dispelled it stays gone until the item is equipped again. The reference schedules the six-hour recast only from a live bundle and a reload restores only saved bundles, so neither a reconnect nor the reroll brings the spell back, and the item stops losing Cast When Held charges meanwhile. Re-equipping casts it and charges the item again. Passive enchantment modifiers are computed directly from equipped items. Artifact effects without a spell icon, such as the Mace of Molag Bal’s borrowed Strength and spell points, are not offered. The Spellbook lists a held spell as lasting while its item is worn rather than as a round count. [Reference reroll](https://github.com/Interkarma/daggerfall-unity/blob/3d85a283672a4955e32992097ea7719e896dbadb/Assets/Scripts/Game/MagicAndEffects/EntityEffectManager.cs) Null Magicka’s installed record says Touch, while the reference picker acts only on the player who cast it. The shared-world catalogue exposes it as self. [Reference dispel](https://github.com/Interkarma/daggerfall-unity/blob/3d85a283672a4955e32992097ea7719e896dbadb/Assets/Scripts/Game/MagicAndEffects/Effects/Mysticism/DispelMagic.cs)
- Banish Daedra and Holy Touch use their installed touch deliveries. Holy Word and custom self-delivered banishment use the reference’s 14 m nearby radius, with a same-floor and visibility restriction in the shared world. Each matching foe rolls separately. Banishment bypasses ordinary damage, death, Soul Trap and loot. Active quest foes must be defeated through their quest’s normal combat route. [Reference nearby objects](https://github.com/Interkarma/daggerfall-unity/blob/3d85a283672a4955e32992097ea7719e896dbadb/Assets/Scripts/Internal/PlayerGPS.cs) · [Reference banishment](https://github.com/Interkarma/daggerfall-unity/blob/3d85a283672a4955e32992097ea7719e896dbadb/Assets/Scripts/Game/MagicAndEffects/Effects/Mysticism/DispelUndead.cs)
- Concealment uses the server’s current effects and the actor’s current movement. The multiplayer appearance uses translucency for stationary Chameleon and moving Shadow. Invisibility hides the figure and nameplate. The reference hides concealed NPC meshes, and its enemy-sight test rolls against Chameleon and Shadow. The shared-world movement rules predate the peer renderer. Peer snapshots publish an appearance field, while timed-effect detail and detection markers go only to their recipient. [Reference entity visibility](https://github.com/Interkarma/daggerfall-unity/blob/3d85a283672a4955e32992097ea7719e896dbadb/Assets/Scripts/Game/Entities/EntityConcealmentBehaviour.cs) · [Reference enemy sight](https://github.com/Interkarma/daggerfall-unity/blob/3d85a283672a4955e32992097ea7719e896dbadb/Assets/Scripts/Game/EnemySenses.cs)

## Buying and casting

A standard spell seller quotes a price for the character at the counter. Four times the buyer’s casting cost gives the base. The Witches’ Festival halves that amount, and the classic trade formula then uses the hall’s quality, Mercantile and Personality. When the price changes before confirmation, the seller displays a fresh quote. A server fixture at quality 14 verified a spell costing 142 gold on an ordinary day and 71 on the festival. Mages Guild identification on that holiday costs zero gold, including for a customer without guild rank. [Spell purchase test](../test/rpg-magic.test.mjs) · [Identification test](../test/rpg-alchemy.test.mjs) · [Reference spell seller](https://github.com/Interkarma/daggerfall-unity/blob/3d85a283672a4955e32992097ea7719e896dbadb/Assets/Scripts/Game/UserInterfaceWindows/DaggerfallSpellBookWindow.cs)

The Mages Guild’s Spell Maker is open to members from rank zero. The School of Kynareth requires rank six in that temple. The server rechecks the live NPC, conversation, reach and rank at purchase. A design has one to three effects, compatible delivery and element, and an icon, with server-computed gold and magicka costs. The purchased design is saved in the owner’s spellbook and is readied immediately. [Maker validation](../server/rpg/magic/spellmaker.js) · [Guild rules](../server/rpg/guilds/rules.js) · [Reference Mages Guild](https://github.com/Interkarma/daggerfall-unity/blob/3d85a283672a4955e32992097ea7719e896dbadb/Assets/Scripts/Game/Guilds/MagesGuild.cs) · [Reference temple gates](https://github.com/Interkarma/daggerfall-unity/blob/3d85a283672a4955e32992097ea7719e896dbadb/Assets/Scripts/Game/Guilds/Temple.cs)

Self, touch and areas around the caster resolve when cast. Ranged spells launch at 25 m/s for up to eight seconds. The server sweeps through geometry and current actor positions. An area missile bursts at the first impact with a 4 m radius and geometry checks to each recipient. Launch and impact are separate events and saves. Private quest recipients are filtered at both steps. The client draws a coloured projectile cue alongside the existing first-person casting animation. ## Verification

- A signed-in Play fixture started at the authored Spell Maker Elanna Hearthhouse in Daggerfall’s Mages Guild. A nonmember was refused. A rank-zero member bought **Baylight** for 64 gold, cast it with V, and recovered the same design after reconnect. A separate cold boot loaded the owner’s design, kept it private from another account, and cast it for 7 magicka. [Play verification](gameplay-coverage.md)
- A private Play fixture set Recall at Elanna’s counter, staged the traveller outside at the hall’s authored exterior landing, and reconnected there. The Recall choice returned the player to the saved coordinates and floor, removed the anchor and preserved the readied Baylight spell. The exterior staging was a fixture checkpoint. The return was the normal spell action. [Recall runtime test](../test/rpg-magic.test.mjs)
- A server fixture using installed geometry and production services locked and opened the same action door in Peristair `0,1:21997`. A second client received the open pose. A lethal hit on a trapped Imp left it at 1 HP when the caster had no empty gem. A subsequent lethal hit filled the caster’s real gem with the Imp’s 1,000-point soul. The fixture also identified an inventory record and cured a disease through the shared inventory and condition services. [Dungeon integration](../test/rpg-dungeon-actions.test.mjs) · [Spell regressions](../test/rpg-magic.test.mjs)
- Regression coverage includes wall impact, an actor moving off the missile path, ranged area occlusion, private quest entities at launch and impact, detection expiry, elemental and paralysis immunity, attributes, missing gems, both door spells waiting at once, and Spell Maker rank and purchase persistence. [Server tests](../test/rpg-magic.test.mjs) · [Client tests](../test/rpg-magic-client.test.mjs)
- A separate Peristair Play fixture used one browser and a second live WebSocket client. Detect Enemy marked nearby foes on the compass and sent zero markers to the other client. The peer cast Invisibility and vanished from the browser view, then cast Null Magicka and returned to view. Holy Word removed a Skeletal Warrior and published a banishment result without a death or kill event. An equipped conjured Steel Dagger survived a reconnect, then vanished from both inventory and equipment after a fixture advance of personal game time. The focused serial run passed all 108 magic, combat, inventory, trade, actor and dungeon-action tests.
