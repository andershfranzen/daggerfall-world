Third-party notices for server/rpg/combat.js and server/rpg/combat/

formulas.js, classes.js, careers.js and monsters.js adapt numbers, tables and formulas from Daggerfall Unity
(https://github.com/Interkarma/daggerfall-unity):
  Assets/Scripts/Utility/EnemyBasics.cs, Assets/Scripts/Utility/RandomEncounters.cs,
  Assets/Scripts/Game/Formulas/FormulaHelper.cs, Assets/Scripts/Game/Entities/EnemyEntity.cs,
  Assets/Scripts/Game/Items/DaggerfallUnityItem.cs, Assets/Scripts/Game/Items/LootTables.cs,
  Assets/Scripts/Game/EnemyAttack.cs, Assets/Scripts/Game/EnemyMotor.cs, Assets/Scripts/Game/WeaponManager.cs,
  Assets/Scripts/Game/Items/ItemHelper.cs (AssignEnemyStartingEquipment), Assets/Scripts/Game/Items/ItemBuilder.cs,
  Assets/Scripts/Internal/DaggerfallMobileUnit.cs and Assets/Scripts/Utility/GameObjectHelper.cs (gendered textures),
  Assets/Scripts/Utility/RDBLayout.cs (classic slot selection, water and passive rules), Assets/Scripts/API/MonsterFile.cs,
  Assets/Scripts/API/DFCareer.cs, Assets/Scripts/Utility/DFRandom.cs, Assets/Scripts/Game/Entities/PlayerEntity.cs
  (IntermittentEnemySpawn), Assets/Scripts/Game/DaedraSeducerMobileBehaviour.cs.
Class careers are read at runtime from the operator's CLASS*.CFG; none ship here.
World of Daggerfall (Nexus 249) encounter markers are read by server/rpg/wilderness.js from the
operator's local pack; combat contains no World of Daggerfall code or data.
No Bethesda art, text or audio is included; sprite references are archive/record
indices resolved from the player's own ARENA2 files.
