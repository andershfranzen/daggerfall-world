Third-party notices for client/gameplay/atmosphere/ and server/rpg/atmosphere.js
==============================================================================

Code in this directory is adapted from two MIT-licensed projects:

- daggerfall-js-source by Lattymoy (checked out locally as .ref/dfjs-research-03):
    src/formats/hmiFile.js          -> hmi.js (HMI song reader, adapted)
    src/formats/sndFile.js          -> engine.js (8-bit PCM handling, DAGGER.SND layout)
    src/systems/gmSynth.js          -> synth.js (GM family grouping, percussion key table)
    src/systems/song/songManager.js -> playlists.js (song lists, day-seeded selection)
    src/systems/gameDate.js         -> calendar.js (calendar arithmetic)
- Daggerfall Unity, https://github.com/Interkarma/daggerfall-unity:
    Utility/DaggerfallDateTime.cs, Internal/WorldTime.cs, Game/Formulas/FormulaHelper.cs
    (rest recovery rates), SoundClips.cs (clip numbers), SongManager.cs (song lists),
    Utility/DFRandom.cs (the LCG used to pick a song for the day).

No Daggerfall game data is included. DAGGER.SND and MIDI.BSA are read from the player's
own ARENA2 folder in the browser and never uploaded. The Elder Scrolls II: Daggerfall is
the property of Bethesda Softworks.
