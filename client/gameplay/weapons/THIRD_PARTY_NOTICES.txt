Third-party notices for client/gameplay/weapons/ and client/gameplay/weapons.js
==============================================================================

The first-person weapon view adapts code from the two MIT-licensed projects
below. Their notices follow verbatim.

- daggerfall-js-source by Lattymoy, https://github.com/Lattymoy/daggerfall-js-source
  src/formats/cifRciFile.js, src/formats/baseImageFile.js and
  src/formats/dfPalette.js (CIF reader, RLE decoder, palette) were adapted
  into cifReader.js. src/combat/fpsWeapon.js (WeaponBasics animation tables,
  weapon file names, screen placement) was adapted into animation.js.

- Daggerfall Unity, https://github.com/Interkarma/daggerfall-unity
  API/CifRciFile.cs, API/BaseImageFile.cs, API/DFPalette.cs,
  Game/FPSWeapon.cs, Game/WeaponBasics.cs and Game/WeaponManager.cs (swing
  gesture sectors, animation stepping), from which the files above were
  translated.

No Daggerfall game data is included. The Elder Scrolls II: Daggerfall is the
property of Bethesda Softworks; WEAPON*.CIF and ART_PAL.COL are read from the
player's own installation in the browser and never leave the device.
