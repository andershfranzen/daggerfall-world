Third-party notices for client/gameplay/actors.js and client/gameplay/actors/
=============================================================================

spriteArchive.js adapts the TEXTURE.nnn reader of daggerfall-js-source
(src/formats/textureFile.js), itself a translation of Daggerfall Unity's
API/TextureFile.cs. tables.js and logic.js transcribe archive numbers,
animation record/flip tables, frame rates, corpse textures and the sprite
scale rule from Daggerfall Unity's Utility/EnemyBasics.cs,
Internal/DaggerfallMobileUnit.cs, Internal/MobilePersonBillboard.cs,
Internal/DaggerfallLootDataTables.cs and DaggerfallUnityEnums.cs.

- Daggerfall Unity, https://github.com/Interkarma/daggerfall-unity
  (consulted at commit 3d85a283672a4955e32992097ea7719e896dbadb)
- daggerfall-js-source by Lattymoy, https://github.com/Lattymoy/daggerfall-js-source
  (commit a3eb1605ea1dc391b691fe314377458a7e63e483)

No Daggerfall game data is included. Sprites are decoded in the browser from
the player's own installation and never leave the device. The Elder Scrolls
II: Daggerfall is the property of Bethesda Softworks.
