client/gameplay/automap — third-party notice

The automap's level slicing (cut away geometry above the player's eye height plus a small bias, and keep lower
geometry visible) follows the behaviour of Daggerfall Unity's automap:
  Assets/Scripts/Game/Automap.cs (UpdateSlicingPositionY)
  Assets/Scripts/Game/UserInterfaceWindows/DaggerfallAutomapWindow.cs (defaultSlicingBiasY = 0.2)
No source code was copied; the implementation in model.js and controller.js is original.
