Third-party notices for client/gameplay/travel/, client/gameplay/travel.js,
client/gameplay/journey.js and client/gameplay/mount/
=============================================================================

catalog.js, journey.js and mapimages.js translate code and tables from
Daggerfall Unity, https://github.com/Interkarma/daggerfall-unity (MIT):
  API/MapsFile.cs (ReadMapTable, LongitudeLatitudeToMapPixel, GetClimateIndex,
  GetPoliticIndex, Climates), API/DFRegion.cs (LocationTypes), API/PakFile.cs,
  API/ImgFile.cs (palette choice, headerless sizes),
  Game/Utility/TravelTimeCalculator.cs (CalculateTravelTime, CalculateTripCost),
  Game/UserInterfaceWindows/DaggerfallTravelPopUp.cs (day rounding, cautious
  arrival) and Game/UserInterfaceWindows/DaggerfallTravelMapWindow.cs
  (GetRegionMapNames, PopulateRegionOffsetDict, GetRegionMapScale, map dot
  palette indices).
client/gameplay/mount/ (cfa.js, rules.js) also translates Daggerfall Unity (MIT):
  API/CfaFile.cs and API/BaseImageFile.cs (CFA header and RLE),
  Game/TransportManager.cs (riding art placement and frame time) and
  Game/Player/PlayerSpeedChanger.cs / PlayerHeightChanger.cs (ride speed and
  rider height).

client/gameplay/journey.js adapts the travel-view camera law of Daggerfall
Online's research snapshot (src/player/travelCamera.js, MIT, Copyright (c)
2026 Lattymoy): a raised, tilted eye over the traveller that never moves the
player, with the traveller's own sprite drawn larger from height. Its licence
text is the MIT text below with that copyright line.

The BSA reader and palette parser are reused from client/world/ (see
client/world/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.txt, which also covers daggerfall-js-source).

No Daggerfall game data is included. Map pictures, place names and coordinates
are read at run time from the player's or operator's own installation.
The Elder Scrolls II: Daggerfall is the property of Bethesda Softworks.
