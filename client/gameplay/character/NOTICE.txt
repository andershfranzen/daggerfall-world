Third-party notices for client/gameplay/character/
==================================================

rules.js, cfg.js and classic.js contain rules and file-format readers adapted
from the projects below. portraits.js follows Daggerfall Unity's head-file mapping
(Game/Entities/RaceTemplate.cs, Game/UserInterface/FacePicker.cs,
API/CifRciFile.cs, Utility/ImageReader.cs) and reuses the CIF reader in
../inventory/images.js (from dfjs src/formats/cifRciFile.js). Both are MIT licensed. Their notices follow verbatim.

- Daggerfall Unity, https://github.com/Interkarma/daggerfall-unity
  Game/Formulas/FormulaHelper.cs, Game/UserInterface/StatsRollout.cs,
  Game/UserInterface/SkillsRollout.cs, Game/Entities/DaggerfallSkills.cs,
  Game/Entities/PlayerEntity.cs, Game/Entities/RaceTemplate.cs,
  Game/UserInterfaceWindows/CreateCharCustomClass.cs,
  Game/UserInterfaceWindows/CreateCharSpecialAdvantageWindow.cs,
  API/ClassFile.cs, API/DFCareer.cs, API/TextFile.cs, API/BiogFile.cs,
  API/BiogFileMCP.cs, Game/UserInterfaceWindows/CreateCharClassQuestions.cs
  (including its question-to-path answer table),
  Game/UserInterfaceWindows/DaggerfallStartNewGameWizard.cs.

- daggerfall-js-source / dfjs by Lattymoy,
  src/systems/chargen.js, src/systems/skills.js, src/formats/classFile.js.

No Daggerfall game data is included. Class records (CLASS*.CFG), the class
questionnaire (TEXT.RSC, CLASSES.DAT), biographies (BIOG*.TXT), faces and other
art are read from the player's or operator's own installation at runtime. The
