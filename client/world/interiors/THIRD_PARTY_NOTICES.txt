Third-party notices for client/world/interiors/, client/gameplay/interiors.js and server/rpg/interiors*
=====================================================================================================

These modules contain code adapted from the two MIT-licensed projects below. Their notices follow verbatim.

- daggerfall-js-source by Lattymoy, https://github.com/Lattymoy/daggerfall-js-source
  (commit a3eb1605ea1dc391b691fe314377458a7e63e483):
    src/formats/blocksFile.js   RMB sub-record (interior) and RDB readers, FixRdbData   -> blocks.js
    src/formats/mapsFile.js     LocationRecordElement and MAPDITEM dungeon reader       -> dungeonMaps.js
    src/world/meshReader.js     door law (subMeshDoorType) and door vertex pass         -> scene.js
    src/world/staticDoors.js    static door centre/normal/size                         -> scene.js
    src/world/interior/interiorLayout.js  interior placement, IsBadInteriorModel table -> layout.js
    src/world/rdbLayout.js      RDB model matrix, action-door and exit-door rules       -> layout.js

- Daggerfall Unity, https://github.com/Interkarma/daggerfall-unity
  (consulted at commit 3d85a283672a4955e32992097ea7719e896dbadb), which the files above translate:
    API/BlocksFile.cs, API/MapsFile.cs, API/DFLocation.cs, MeshReader.cs, Utility/GameObjectHelper.cs,
    Utility/RDBLayout.cs, Utility/RMBLayout.cs, Internal/DaggerfallInterior.cs, Internal/DaggerfallDungeon.cs,
    Internal/DaggerfallStaticDoors.cs, Game/PlayerEnterExit.cs (landing rules). RDB action decoding and
    linked movement also refer to API/DFBlock.cs, Internal/DaggerfallAction.cs, Internal/DaggerfallActionDoor.cs
    and Game/Questing/Place.cs (quest markers).
    buildingDirectory.js, buildingWords.js and nameBanks.js use GenerateBuildingName in FormulaHelper.cs,
    RMBLayout.GetLocationBuildingData, the English name fragment lists from Internal_Strings.csv,
    the NAMEGEN.DAT format and DFRandom. Building seeds and personal names come from the local ARENA2.

No Daggerfall game data is included. The Elder Scrolls II: Daggerfall is the property of Bethesda Softworks;
its files are read from the player's own installation in the browser, or from the server operator's own
ARENA2 directory for validation, and are never distributed.
