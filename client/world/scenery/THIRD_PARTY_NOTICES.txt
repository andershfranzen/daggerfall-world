Third-party notices for client/world/scenery/ and client/gameplay/scenery*
=========================================================================

rmbFlats.js, flatTextures.js, sky.js, terrain.js, colliders.js and index.js in this directory
(and client/gameplay/scenery/pass.js, which draws their output) contain code
and constants adapted from the two MIT-licensed projects below. Their notices
follow verbatim.

- Daggerfall Unity, https://github.com/Interkarma/daggerfall-unity
  (consulted at commit 3d85a283672a4955e32992097ea7719e896dbadb)
  API/BlocksFile.cs, API/TextureFile.cs, API/SkyFile.cs, API/WoodsFile.cs,
  API/MapsFile.cs, API/DFLocation.cs, MeshReader.cs, MaterialReader.cs,
  Utility/RMBLayout.cs, Utility/ClimateSwaps.cs, Utility/RDBLayout.cs,
  Utility/TextureReader.cs, Internal/DaggerfallSky.cs,
  Internal/DaggerfallBillboard.cs, Terrain/TerrainNature.cs,
  Terrain/DefaultTerrainSampler.cs, Terrain/TerrainTexturing.cs, and the
  tree/rock/plant/prop groups of
  Assets/Game/Addons/RmbBlockEditor/Editor/Persisted/DefaultFlatsCatalog.json
  (colliders.js).
  client/render/sky-dome.js and the night-sky reader in sky.js also follow
  Internal/DaggerfallSky.cs (the sky frame for the time of day, the NITEnnI0.IMG
  chosen for a SKY set, stars over the night image's open-sky indices) and
  Utility/DaggerfallDateTime.cs (the moons' 32-day cycle and day offsets).

- daggerfall-js-source by Lattymoy, https://github.com/Lattymoy/daggerfall-js-source
  (commit a3eb1605ea1dc391b691fe314377458a7e63e483): src/formats/skyFile.js,
  textureFile.js, woodsFile.js and blocksFile.js.

No Daggerfall game data is included. The Elder Scrolls II: Daggerfall is the
property of Bethesda Softworks; its files are read from the player's own
installation in the browser and never leave the device.
